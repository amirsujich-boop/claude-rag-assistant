# Returns and warranty

You can return an unused product within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. The product has to be in its original packaging. Shipping for returns is paid by the customer unless the product arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days after we receive the product.

## Warranty

All devices come with a 24-month warranty. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover damage from drops, liquids or unauthorised repairs.

To make a warranty claim, send the order number and a short description of the problem through the contact form. We reply within one working day with the next steps.
