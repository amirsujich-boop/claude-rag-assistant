# Opening hours and contact

The showroom is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday from 9:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Phone support follows the same hours. Outside those hours you can leave a message through the website form and we reply on the next working day.

## Address

Example Street 12, 71000 Sarajevo. Parking is available behind the building; the entrance is from the side street.
