# Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are shipped the same day. Delivery inside the country takes 1-2 working days. Delivery to EU countries takes 3-6 working days.

Shipping is free for orders above 100 EUR. Below that, shipping costs 6 EUR inside the country and 15 EUR to EU countries.

You receive a tracking number by email as soon as the parcel is handed to the courier.
